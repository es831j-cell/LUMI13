# LUMI APPROVED VISUAL REFERENCE

Status: CANONICAL
Project line: Lumi 2.0+
Approval rule: The owner's phrase **"lock it in"** is the canonical approval signal. When a later locked visual replaces an earlier locked visual, the later locked visual is authoritative.

## Canonical Visual

Lumi's approved visual identity is a **single layered spherical energy orb**.

### Required form
- Perfectly spherical overall silhouette.
- Dark metallic segmented outer shell / structural ribs.
- Multiple translucent, glass-like inner spherical layers.
- Strong luminous central nucleus.
- Visible internal depth, refraction, reflections, and layered energy structure.
- Premium dimensional appearance with substantial visual mass.
- Subtle internal particles / energy detail are allowed when they reinforce depth rather than clutter the silhouette.
- The orb must read as one coherent engineered energy system, not as loose orbital lines.

### Surface and lighting
- Dark graphite / black metallic structural elements.
- Clear or smoky translucent glass layers.
- Bright internal light concentrated toward the core.
- Violet, indigo, blue and white are the baseline identity palette unless a runtime state explicitly changes the active-state lighting.
- Rim light, bloom and internal reflections should enhance depth without washing out shell geometry.

### Motion
- Gentle, continuous three-dimensional motion is allowed.
- Internal layers may rotate or drift at different rates.
- Motion should feel calm and alive, not like a wireframe demo or spinning logo.
- The bright central core should remain visually stable enough to preserve identity while surrounding layers move.

### Runtime-state behavior
- Listening, Thinking, Speaking and Idle must remain visually distinguishable.
- State changes should alter illumination, energy intensity, motion or state coloration while preserving the exact orb geometry and material identity.
- State rendering must never substitute a different avatar geometry.

## Visual quality gate
A phone build fails the visual gate if the displayed visual lacks the canonical orb's layered shell geometry, translucent depth, dark structural ribs, or strong luminous central core, even if the overall silhouette is spherical.

A thin line sphere, sparse orbital model, generic glowing ball, flat logo, or other low-depth approximation does not satisfy this reference.

## Change control
- This file is the active visual source of truth.
- A replacement visual becomes canonical only after an explicit **"lock it in"** approval.
- Until that phrase is given for a new visual, new renders are candidates only.
- Renderer work must preserve already verified non-visual phone capabilities unless a visual change strictly requires otherwise.
